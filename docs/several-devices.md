# Several devices

You can use one Conest account on several devices, for example a phone
and a computer. Each device has its own keys; your contacts accept
messages from all of them, and send to each.

## Linking a device

1. Set up Conest on the new device as usual, then open Settings >
   Devices > "Link this device to my account". It shows a QR code.
2. On a device already on the account, open Settings > Devices and scan
   that code (or paste it).
3. Both devices show a six-digit code. Check that they match, then
   confirm.

A device linked less than a day ago cannot add or remove other devices
yet. Any device can remove a lost one (Settings > Devices).

## What is shared today

- Messages you send in direct chats, and your Saved messages texts.
- Which messages are delivered and read.

Not yet: messages you receive while only one device can be reached,
files, voice messages, groups and settings. These are being added next.

## Devices out of touch

A device that has not been in touch with your other devices for a while
(7 days by default, Settings > Devices) is marked unconfirmed, and
nothing is synced with it until you confirm it on a device that stayed in
touch. Your contacts see a similar note on messages from a device of
yours that your other devices no longer list.
