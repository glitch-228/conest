## M37 follow-up findings

M37 was restored to its original `de74426` and its worktree is clean. Its independent review recorded two Linux Bluetooth reliability risks in `native/conest_native/src/ble/linux.rs`, unchanged by M35b:

- First-write admission: the GATT callback requires an existing peripheral link, reserved when the main loop consumes the notification event. A central can subscribe and send its first handshake before that event is consumed, receiving NotPermitted. Review suggested a bounded admission wait and a timing-focused native test.
- Cancellation cleanup: the central worker disconnects at its normal async tail, which is skipped if its future is dropped/aborted. Disabling the mesh or retrying may leave an OS Bluetooth connection active. Review suggested explicit cancellation cleanup and adapter-level qualification.

These require separate native/BlueZ qualification; they are not claimed fixed or reproduced on hardware here.
