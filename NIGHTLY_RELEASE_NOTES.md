## Conest 0.3.12 nightly (October 14)

This nightly lets you choose how much of Conest you see. Everything from
the October 13 nightlies (messages on several routes, Delta Chat, files
without metadata) is included.

### Before you update

- Nothing changes for an existing install: it stays in Advanced mode,
  which shows everything, as before. You can switch at the top of
  Settings → Personal.

### Simple, Standard and Advanced (new)

- After creating a new identity, you choose a mode:
  - Simple: one list of chats, larger text, and only the personal
    settings (Show all settings brings back the rest).
  - Standard: like the messengers you know. On a phone, Chats, Calls and
    Settings are tabs at the bottom; the Calls tab lists recent calls.
  - Advanced: everything, including the routes each message took and the
    route tests in a contact's profile.
- In Simple and Standard mode, messages show sent, delivered and read
  without routes, and message details give delivery and read times (per
  member in groups).

### Not tested on devices yet

- The three modes on a phone and on a desktop, including the Calls tab.
